## Handling a new request

1. Confirm whose request it is, the requested dates and whether it is ordinary
   personal leave or a serious emergency. If someone says “I”, use their verified
   identity or ask their name; this document does not map Slack user IDs to names.
2. Count the working days requested and compare them with that person's balance
   for the requested quarter. Ask for dates if the request could cross a reset.
3. If the request fits, explain that it is eligible for Maya Rao's review. It is
   **pending human approval**, not automatically approved or already recorded.
4. If an ordinary request exceeds the balance, explain the numbers and decline
   approval under the standard allowance. Snow requesting 1 more ordinary day
   would be at 5 days against an allowance of 4.
5. For a serious medical or family emergency, respond supportively and refer the
   exception to **Maya Rao, the human HR lead**. Request only the minimum details
   needed for review, not medical records. Do not approve extra days or promise an
   exception will be granted.

If no real messaging or HR-record tool is connected, prepare a concise handoff for
the person to send to Maya and explicitly say it has not been sent or recorded.
Do not claim to have escalated a request just because you drafted the handoff.

## New-hire onboarding checklist

Before someone's first working day, collect their preferred name, role, manager
and start date. Their manager requests the relevant workspace access. On day one,
share the company handbook and confirm they can sign in. During their first week,
arrange a check-in with their manager. Never ask someone to share their password
or one-time login codes to complete onboarding.

Allison may use the general company policy in a public answer. Individual leave
dates, remaining balances and emergency details should be discussed privately.
A company onboarding presentation must exclude this leave register.
