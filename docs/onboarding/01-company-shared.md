# Atlas company handbook

This is fictional company data for an OpenHuman demonstration. People, policies,
customers and numbers below are demo fixtures, not real employment records.

## What Atlas does

Atlas makes project software for small teams. Customers organise projects, assign
tasks and track deadlines in a workspace. Starter customers use email and password.
Enterprise customers use single sign-on (SSO): their company manages their sign-in
through its identity provider.

Our working hours are Monday–Friday, 10:00–18:00, Asia/Kolkata. Saturday and Sunday
are non-working days. Do not promise a response or resolution outside these hours.

## Company-wide leave policy

Each employee receives **4 paid personal-leave working days per calendar quarter**.
The current quarter is **1 October–31 December 2026**. Balances reset on 1 January
2027; unused days do not carry over. A full working day counts as 1 day. Weekends
do not use this allowance. No public-holiday calendar is supplied in this demo.

Available days equal the quarterly allowance minus personal-leave days already
approved and taken in that quarter. Pending or cancelled requests do not count as
leave already taken. Check the employee's HR record before quoting their balance.

Requests within the allowance still require Maya Rao's approval. Ordinary requests
that exceed the allowance cannot be approved under this policy. A serious medical
or family emergency may be considered by Maya as an exception; it is not an
automatic approval or an automatic addition to the allowance.

Individual leave histories and balances belong to HR. Do not infer someone's
balance from the allowance alone or publish employee records in a shared channel.

## Communication standards

- Explain the relevant rule, the next useful step and any missing information.
- Keep customer replies short, polite and specific to the reported issue.
- Never request passwords, one-time login codes or API keys.
- Obtain human approval for refunds and leave exceptions.
- Distinguish a drafted response from a sent message, and a proposed request from
  a recorded or approved one. Report an action as completed only when it actually
  succeeded in a connected system.
